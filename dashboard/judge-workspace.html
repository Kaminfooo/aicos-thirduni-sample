    Evaluation scope & credits

    Understand the outputs

    Reference examples demonstrate the workflow, not guaranteed accuracy. Smoke can be missed indoors. Violence and interaction research previews can miss incidents or raise false alerts; reliable recognition across scenes is not established. Review the surrounding footage and site policy before acting. Software checks and recognition measurements are separate evidence.

    Test safely

    Use the supplied controlled simulations or permitted recordings. Never stage fire, violence or a fall. Browser camera access requires localhost or HTTPS and explicit permission. Selected detector groups process sampled frames; some events may occur between samples.
